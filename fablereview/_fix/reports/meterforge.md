- [review #1 / backlog 3.8] Rewrote `02-CONCEPTS.md` §7 "Moving this design to SQL" from three paragraphs of generalities into the concrete bridge the review asked for:
  - The `usage_events` DDL with `CONSTRAINT usage_events_idempotency UNIQUE (tenant_id, external_event_id)`, plus the point that the constraint is compound *because* the dictionary key is `(TenantId, ExternalEventId)` — and that indexing on `external_event_id` alone would surface as a unique violation on a legitimate cross-tenant request (which `Ingest_SameExternalIdInDifferentTenants_AcceptsBoth` is the test that catches).

It spells out the trap that `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING RETURNING *` returns **zero rows** on conflict, and contrasts the `DO UPDATE SET id = id` variant with its cost (a write and a row lock on every replay).
  - A six-row table of "what changes when the winner comes back from Postgres": reference vs. value comparison, read-your-write and cross-replica visibility, uncertain commit and why retrying is safe here, `DbUpdateException`/`SqlState 23505` and matching on the **constraint name** rather than "any unique violation", restart durability, and side effects that must commit with the insert and must not repeat on a replay.
  - Closes by noting the CTE form also tells you *whether* a row was created, which is what a correct outbox write needs.
